Lab topology

Every client request goes to one BNK VIP. One iRule per endpoint checks the key, scans the prompt, applies the quota and schedule, picks the backend and meters the answer, for the T4 GPUs and for Amazon Bedrock alike. Solid lines are customer traffic (data plane), dashed lines are configuration (control plane). Pick a flow to watch a request travel, or click any box to see what it does.
